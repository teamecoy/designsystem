Global · callouts

Callouts

A callout is the offer on the brand's eight-lobe flower shape (the one you sent). It's campaign artwork for ads, emails and banners only. The website's pills, badges and their colours stay exactly as they are; nothing on the site's product cards changes for Christmas.

Decided, and what's still open

  1. Decided · accent rule: Off White ground uses red and green type, Deep Green uses White and Light Green, red uses White and Light Pink, Light Pink uses Burgundy and Deep Green.
  2. Decided · live is Deep Green: no red main grounds in launch. Labels, panels and bands are Deep Green; red is only buttons, callouts, small tags and accents.
  3. Decided · last chance from Wed 16 Dec: Light Pink is the main ground; Burgundy is the paper and the small elements. Labels on red paper turn Off White. The date goes into the pill. Free express shipping is Wed 16 Dec, per the calendar.
  4. Decided · how the set is tied: white satin ribbon and bow on the top piece; a white ribbon over the side end of each lower piece, never across the front. New colours use FLOATING-RIBBON-PROMPT.md in the design system.
  5. Decided · names: "callout" for the offer on the flower shape; "formats" for the five creative approaches.
  6. Decided · site badges untouched: website pills and badges stay as they are.
  7. Decided · doodles: arrows and hand-drawn marks come from the design system's glyph library only.
  8. Decided · Tote colour: shoppers choose their Tote colour.
  9. Open · Tote: stock after Black Friday phase 2, and whether "Worth $70" is the real value.
  10. Open · shipping cutoff: the 18 Dec date still needs checking against Malpa's guidance.

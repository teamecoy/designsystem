## Copy guardrails

- Never lead with the discount depth. Headlines are gifting lines; the offer sits in the pill or callout.
- The Tote is a separate gift: never say the order arrives packed in it.
- Say "Free Tote Bag on $300+" (or "on orders $300+"); where there's room, add "after your BOGO discount".
- Don't promise delivery dates beyond "Order by Fri 18 Dec" until the Malpa check is done.
- No claims the brand can't back (see `../../BRAND.md` and the bamboo claims guardrails): no odour, organic, hypoallergenic or antimicrobial claims.
- "What not to say" and the approved headline list are still TBC in the brief; the board's approved lines are in IDENTITY.md §7.

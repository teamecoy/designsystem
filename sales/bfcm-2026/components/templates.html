Phase colour: the one rule

Put one phase class on every frame: .bf26--hype (LED white), .bf26--live (LED green #77D686; Day 1, main sale, win-back, GWP 2) or .bf26--end (FOMO colour, TBA; Bright Orange holds). The class recolours the headline, the accent offer line, the value tickers and the stamps. Offers stay white. From the PGS phase pages and Nick's draft statics (19 Oct); the statics are work in progress and get a resync.

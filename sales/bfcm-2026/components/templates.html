Phase colour: the one rule

Put one phase class on every frame: .bf26--hype (LED white), .bf26--live (LED green #77D686; Day 1, main sale, win-back, GWP 2) or .bf26--end (LED orange #FF6301, back on black fabric). The class recolours the headline, the accent offer line, the value tickers, the stamps and the badges. Offers stay white.

BFCM 2026 · type and callouts

Circular callouts

LED text set on a circle, the phrase repeated twice. Live text, so it can say anything short. The phase accent on dark grounds, white on orange. Keep one per piece. It can overlap a corner of the headline (Nick's PDP option 1) but never cover the offer.

Badges · colour follows the phase

Nick's starburst badges are the callout device for benefits and offers. .bf26-burst takes its colour from the phase class; --merlot, --white, --ink, --green and --orange pick an alternate. Clash caps, two or three short lines, tilted. Mix at most two colours in one piece.

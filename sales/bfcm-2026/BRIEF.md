# BFCM 2026 · offer and copy rules

The parts of the agency brief that change what goes on a design. **The source is James's Notion page, "Black Friday 2026 — Sale Brief"** (https://app.notion.com/p/3e3c6b9ddf0481b18431f5fe05665efd). This is a snapshot of it as last edited on 22 Sep 2026. If the two disagree, Notion wins: re-read it and update this file. Pricing and offer changes go through James.

Look and feel are in [IDENTITY.md](IDENTITY.md). This file is what to say, and when.

## The sale

**One sale, Black Friday 2026: Thu 5 Nov to Mon 30 Nov (Cyber Monday), AEST.** "Cyber Monday" is the last day of this sale, not a separate event: the Cyber Monday headline runs on Mon 30 Nov only.

## Offer by phase

| Phase | Dates | Headline offer | What creative may say | Ticker messages |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hype** | Mon 2 to Wed 4 Nov | None yet | Day 1 shoppers get a special offer; get your code before the sale. Countdown. Hype 1/3 tease, 2/3 calendar invite, 3/3 last chance to get the code. | COMING SOON · GET YOUR DAY 1 CODE |
| **Launch, Day 1** | Thu 5 Nov | **Up to 54% off**, plus an **extra 10% off with the Day 1 code** (up to 64%, the deepest deal of the sale) | Public creative: "Up to 54% off, plus an extra 10% off today with your Day 1 code". Only hype emails, lead gen, the pop-up and the reminder email show the code itself. | DAY 1 ONLY · EXTRA 10% OFF · SALE IS LIVE |
| **Main sale** | Fri 6 to Thu 26 Nov | Up to 54% off storewide | GWP tiers, proof, product | SALE IS LIVE · UP TO 54% OFF · STOREWIDE |
| **Ending: BFCM weekend** | Fri 27 to Sun 29 Nov | **Up to 60% off**, a step up from 54% | Offer escalation: "~~Up to 54% off~~ Now up to 60% off storewide" (IDENTITY.md §5). Black Friday headline. | ENDING SOON · LAST CHANCE · UP TO 60% OFF |
| **Ending: Cyber Monday** | Mon 30 Nov | Up to 60% off, final day | Cyber Monday headline, orange ground or orange LED, last-chance callouts | LAST CHANCE · ENDS TONIGHT · UP TO 60% OFF |

Discounts are tiered by product grade (A, B, C, Sale Bait), from 15% to 54% (60% on BFCM weekend). Always say **"up to"**. Never state one percentage as if it applied to everything.

The 60% step is marked confirmed in the Notion brief. Sam described it on 2026-09-28 as available "if we need to push", so check with James before briefing ending-phase creative.

A $350+ order gets all three. The Notion brief just says "Pillow"; Sam confirmed on 2026-09-28 that it is the Flip Cooling Pillow. Nick's mock-ups say "Free Flip Cooling Pillow worth up to $119 when you spend $300"; that's placeholder copy, and the brief's tiers replace it.

## Copy guardrails: never

- Put the Day 1 code in public creative (ads, site, launch page, organic). Emails, lead gen, the pop-up and the reminder email only. **No code in Meta, TikTok or Google ads.**
- Promise the extra 10% to everyone. It is earned by joining the hype list ("earned access, not a giveaway").
- Pre-announce the 60% step, or the Tote Bag swap on 19 Nov, before it happens.
- Mention the secret bundle (4-item orders get an extra 10%, email subscribers only, after Day 1) anywhere but email.
- Say the code auto-applies. Customers type it in.

## Copy guardrails: never

- Put the Day 1 code in public creative (ads, public site pages, launch page, organic). It appears only on the **hidden hype landing pages**, hype emails, lead gen and the reminder email. **No code in Meta, TikTok or Google ads**: hype ads say "tap to get your code" and land on the hidden page. Codes: `BFEXTRA10` (web, email, ads landing page) and `BFCMEXTRA10` (direct mail variant of the hidden page).
- Put the code in the sale-updates sign-up form. Its success state confirms they're on the list and never reveals a code; the code lives on the page itself.
- Promise the extra 10% to everyone. It is earned by joining the hype list ("earned access, not a giveaway").
- Pre-announce the 60% step, or the Tote Bag swap on 19 Nov, before it happens.
- Mention the secret bundle (4-item orders get an extra 10%, email subscribers only, after Day 1) anywhere but email. No site creative for it.
- Say the code auto-applies. Customers type it in.

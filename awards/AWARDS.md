# Awards

Third-party proof badges. Use one whenever creative says or needs **"award-winning"**, **"award winner"**, **"3-time winner"**, **"as recognised by"**, or a proof line or trust signal that needs backing. That covers ads, emails, web heroes, PDP trust rows and landing pages. Demo: [components/awards.html](../components/awards.html).

The badges are Bedbuyer's artwork, not Ecoy's. Use them **exactly as supplied**: don't recolour, redraw, re-set the type, crop, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows, and don't change the years. Give each one clear space of at least 25% of its height on every side.

### Which one to pick

1. **Off White, white or Light Pink ground**: use a colour version. Prefer the 3-year stacked version; it is the stronger claim. Avoid Light Green and Light Blue, because the badge's teal blends into them. Use an Off White card there instead.
2. **Dark ground or photography**: use a white version. No white 3-year version exists. To show all three years on a dark design, put the colour 3-year badge on an Off White card or pillar shape. Never put a colour badge straight onto a dark colour or a busy photo, because the teal and peach lose contrast.
3. **Height under ~120px** (bars, footers, trust rows): the horizontal version, because the ribbons in the stacked versions get too small to read.
4. **One badge per piece.** It is proof that supports the message, not the hero. Keep it smaller than the headline and the Ecoy logo, usually in a corner or next to the CTA or the reviews.

### Copy that goes with it

- Safe: "Award-winning", "Award-winning bamboo bedding", "3-time Bedbuyer award winner", "Bedbuyer award winner 2023, 2024 & 2025".
- "3-time" or "three years running" only goes with a 3-year badge, or with no badge at all. Don't pair it with a 2025-only badge.
- **TBC:** the award category hasn't been confirmed (for example, which product or "best X"). Don't name a category or a product ("award-winning cooling sheets") as *the* winning product until Sam confirms it. Existing creative already says "award-winning cooling sheets" and "award-winning bamboo bedding".
- Sales: badges can go inside a sale identity as a proof tile. The badge itself is never recoloured to fit the sale palette.

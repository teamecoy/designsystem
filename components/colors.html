Colours · guide p17–p19

Calm where it matters, expressive where it counts

Ten colours. Grounded, natural tones paired with bolder expressive ones. Pair rich, deep tones with light neutrals for contrast; use bright colours as accents, never as foundations. Softer pairings lead in product, editorial and functional contexts; bolder combinations are for campaigns.

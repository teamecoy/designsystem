One typeface, one clear voice

Malinton leads everywhere by default. Satoshi is a tool, not a second personality: allowed when size gets small, information density increases, or legibility is critical. Inter is the Google-safe stand-in for Satoshi and is mandatory in EDM. Leading 120% (raise to 140% when legibility needs it). Tracking −2% on all text.

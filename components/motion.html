Motion design thinking · guide p32–p33

Calm, considered, human

Motion is an extension of the brand, not decoration. It reinforces meaning, guides attention and creates moments that feel natural rather than mechanical. Every movement is purposeful, soft and intuitive. Never rushed, never rigid.

Four principles

Natural over mechanical

Organic and relaxed, the way the products support rest. Ease in, build gently, slow naturally to a stop.

Always easing curves (ease-in-out), never linear.

Purposeful movement

Introduce content, highlight features, guide the eye. Support understanding, never distract from it.

If it doesn't add clarity or hierarchy, it isn't needed.

Soft timing and restraint

Unhurried. Elements arrive calmly and settle smoothly, mirroring better sleep and balance.

Short, subtle animations over long or exaggerated ones.

Consistency across elements

Text, feature chips and UI share one motion language so digital touchpoints feel cohesive.

Same easing logic and timing everywhere.

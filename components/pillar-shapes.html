The visual foundation of the brand

Five scalloped shapes that translate the sleep pillars (Climate, Comfort, Light, Function) into a flexible, recognisable system. They frame content, support layouts or act as bold visual moments. Scale, crop, stack or gently distort them, as long as the core form stays recognisable.

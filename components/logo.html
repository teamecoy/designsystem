Logo · guide p11–p15

A word mark with room to breathe

Bold, rounded forms that reflect softness, warmth and confidence. Always clear, uncluttered and given space. Dark logo on light grounds, light logo on dark grounds; black or white where contrast is limited. Over photography, place it in areas of low visual noise. Clarity comes first: if a colour reduces readability, don't use it.

  2. Dark logo on light colour backgrounds (Deep Green shown; Burgundy on Light Pink is also used on p14)
  3. Monochrome (black or white) for high-contrast applications
  4. Over photography, only where legibility is clearly maintained; apply a colour overlay or neutral treatment if needed

Clear space

Minimum clear space on every side is 100% of the logo height, for every configuration. Test at intended size in real use before shipping.

Common errors

  • Never reduce opacity or add transparency
  • Never stretch or compress
  • No visual effects or shadows
  • Keep it horizontal, no rotation
  • Never outline the logo
  • Never re-set "Ecoy" in another typeface

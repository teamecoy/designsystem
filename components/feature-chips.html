Feature chips

Product-attribute chips support how the brand shows up in digital and product-led environments. Stack, group or place them contextually over imagery and layouts. They should support the message, not dominate it. Keep copy short and scannable, spacing consistent, and never overcrowd one space.

Rules

  • Keep copy short and scannable, two or three words
  • Maintain consistent styling and spacing within a group
  • Avoid overcrowding or too many chips in one space
  • Chips are descriptive. Urgency, value and newness belong to tags, not chips

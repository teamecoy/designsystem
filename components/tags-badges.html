Tags, chips and badges · spec v0.1

One colour per job, one spec per placement

Every tag belongs to exactly one category, and the category sets the colour. No exceptions. Three placements, three specs. All tags set in Satoshi (Inter in EDM), tracking −2%, line height 100%. Sentence case everywhere. Values are proposed defaults pending audit against the live site.

05 · Readability

Functional text on chips targets 4.5:1. White on Bright Orange is the one accepted brand exception (badges only, labels short, minimum 12px Semi Bold, never for functional or body text). Ratios below are computed live from the tokens.

07 · Do and don't

Do

  • Max one on-image badge per card, plus max one inline tag at price level
  • Use the category colour, always
  • Keep labels to two words max
  • Check contrast on imagery before shipping

Don't

  • Stack Sale + Last chance on the same card (pick one)
  • Put newness in burgundy or orange
  • Mix caps and sentence case across placements
  • Restyle a filter pill as a badge or vice versa
